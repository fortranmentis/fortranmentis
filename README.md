## Hi, I'm fortranmentis 👋

I am a **Data Engineer at GeoSR**, working with **marine and meteorological data**, scientific pipelines, forecast analytics, fisheries statistics and geospatial workflows.

**Credentials:** Engineer Meteorology (기상기사) · Engineer Ocean Environment (해양환경기사) · Weather Forecaster License (기상예보사).

I build data workflows, spatial applications, and developer tools, with a focus on **climate variability, ocean and atmospheric datasets, and geospatial analysis**. My work spans climate-data statistics, scientific file processing, analytical reporting, tool-calling AI agents, indexed location search, native mobile interfaces for data and server workflows, sensor–forecast integration, and dispatch-simulation prototypes.

**Repository update · November 12, 2026:** added V-PASS Spatial Analytics and the Pocket Weather Station product website, and refreshed aggregate public/private language statistics. Implementation descriptions do not imply that every feature has shipped to app stores.

I care about the details that make data useful: coordinate systems, temporal coverage, missing values, explicit acceptance rules, and a clear distinction between what a result shows and what still needs validation.

### 🌊 [Ocean Forecast GeoPipeline](https://github.com/fortranmentis/ocean-forecast-geopipeline)

**Connect recurring ocean and sea-ice products to geospatial services.**

Validates incoming NetCDF/CSV files against supplied specifications, regrids and reprojects raster products, derives current speed/direction from U/V components, updates PostgreSQL, and publishes selected GeoServer layers. Field-level reports retain the basis for acceptance decisions.

**Make vessel movement patterns inspectable through rules and spatial context.**

Combines AIS trajectories, vessel metadata, and fairway/anchorage polygons to visualize stationary states, fairway exits, and candidate activities. ### 🐟 [V-PASS Spatial Analytics](https://github.com/fortranmentis/vpass-spatial-analytic)

**Turn vessel observations into traceable fisheries-related spatial statistics.**

Batch and spatial workflows for **2023–2025 V-PASS records**: streaming annual merges, explicit speed/vessel-ID selection, monthly and seasonal counts, regional clipping, and observation-count aggregation to existing grids. Weighted Quartic KDE, Jenks classification, **QGIS common-break styling**, and catch-grade joins connect tabular processing to maps while retaining rejection audits, assignment reports and count reconciliation.

**Observation counts, unique vessels and KDE intensity are different measures.** These outputs do not independently establish fishing effort or catch productivity. The repository contains code and documentation; full reproduction requires locally supplied data, reference layers and the QGIS runtime. [Methods](https://github.com/fortranmentis/vpass-spatial-analytic/blob/main/docs/METHODOLOGY.md) · [Validation scope](https://github.com/fortranmentis/vpass-spatial-analytic/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

### 🌏 [Climate Variability Analysis](https://github.com/fortranmentis/climate-variability-analysis_Jeonbuk-National-University)

**Explore statistical relationships across snow, ocean, and atmosphere.**

A research and coursework archive associated with Jeonbuk National University, examining Jeonju snowfall/snow depth alongside sea surface temperature, atmospheric circulation, and sea ice. Fortran programs, GrADS scripts, and MATLAB routines cover regression, composites, EOF analysis, filtering, and spectral methods.

The **2021 coauthored undergraduate thesis** connects 1983–2021 Jeonju snow observations with NOAA reanalysis through seasonal means, lagged regression and LBM response comparisons. The thesis reports an ENSO association for maximum snow depth and a weaker relation for snow days. [Original thesis, presentation and figures](https://github.com/fortranmentis/climate-variability-analysis_Jeonbuk-National-University#thesis) are linked beside the code.

The [validation record](https://github.com/fortranmentis/climate-variability-analysis_Jeonbuk-National-University/blob/main/VALIDATION.md) documents a limited regression execution check. Full scientific replication, GrADS figure regeneration, and MATLAB execution remain unverified.

### 📍 [Multi-Model Grid Validator](https://github.com/fortranmentis/multi-model-grid-validator)

**Match service locations to nearby model cells that meet explicit value rules.**

Handles **15 model configurations** across ocean, wave, and atmospheric datasets. Selects the nearest eligible cell within **40 km**; non-WRF entries screen the first **up to 20 timesteps**, while WRF entries use coordinate-based matching. Parallel processing combines results into a location-level CSV.

### 🧭 [Marine Travel AI Agent · 바다결](https://github.com/fortranmentis/marine-travel-AI-agent)

**Turn public coastal data into a travel draft whose inputs and decisions can be inspected.**

**GeoSR · Internal technology development.** A **v0.9 local prototype** for one- to three-day car or walking itineraries across **29 forecast-linked scopes**. Eight typed Ollama tools connect tourism, marine forecasts, weather and transport evidence to a deterministic Python planning engine. Form requests now run through **LangGraph: build → validate → traveler choice → recheck → finish**, with at most two rechecks and fixed-booking preservation. SQLite checkpoints and revisioned plans support reload/restart recovery within retention limits; **n8n** schedules data collection, while **Cytoscape and Langfuse** expose filtered execution events. Route maps, source timestamps, visit edits/undo and PDF exports keep drafts reviewable.

Itineraries remain provisional. Conditional island plans require separate round-trip access checks; operator availability, reservations, age limits, warning bulletins, and on-site access are not established by the draft. The [validation record](https://github.com/fortranmentis/marine-travel-AI-agent/blob/main/VALIDATION.md) distinguishes offline tests from live-provider observations.

### 🖥️ [GPUTERM](https://github.com/fortranmentis/GPUTERM)

**Bring remote server work into one desktop workspace.**

Combines split SSH/local terminals, SFTP transfers, and live system/GPU telemetry. Includes monitoring adapters for Ollama, vLLM, and other LLM runtimes, resource history and alerts, reusable SSH keys, and container/Kubernetes and PostgreSQL/MySQL monitoring where supported by the remote environment. Remote web-service discovery opens TensorBoard, Jupyter, MLflow, Ray, Streamlit, Gradio and code-server through temporary loopback SSH forwarding. Read-only MCP discovery omits login tokens and credential-bearing MLflow URIs. Built with Rust, Tauri and React; desktop releases remain beta.

### 🚚 [Truck Dispatch Simulator](https://github.com/fortranmentis/truck-dispatch-simulator)

**Explore how dispatch order and intervals change truck turnaround.**

A Python/Tkinter prototype models empty-bottle and empty trucks through inspection, unloading, loading, and outbound stages. Scenario inputs produce stage-level timestamps, loading groups, and per-truck / truck-type mean turnaround times. Original requirements, source iterations, and Excel references keep the assumptions traceable.

The current scheduler has reproducible resource conflicts, including capacity violations in its supplied example. Outputs are **prototype calculations, not validated operational schedules**; see the [diagnostic evidence](https://github.com/fortranmentis/truck-dispatch-simulator/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

### 📱 GPUTERM for iOS & Android

**Extend remote compute workflows to native mobile workspaces.**

Native SSH/SFTP clients with system/GPU telemetry and LLM runtime monitoring. The iOS app connects **SwiftUI to Rust through UniFFI**; the Android app connects **Kotlin/Jetpack Compose to Rust through JNI**. The native implementations include split terminals, SFTP queues, history and alerts, reusable SSH keys, and container/database monitoring. iOS uses SwiftTerm and Keychain; Android uses native terminal rendering and Keystore-backed credential handling with adaptive phone/tablet/desktop layouts. Recent native workflows add **remote web-service discovery on Linux/macOS/Windows**, temporary SSH forwarding, device-authenticated app/credential locks, and opt-in configuration sync. iOS offers iPad/external-display workspaces and **Siri/App Shortcuts**; Android supports adaptive/external-display sessions and folder-based sync. Passwords and private keys remain device-local. Android publication remains release preparation; no Google Play availability is claimed.

### 🗺️ HelpPoopy / Korea Toilet Map

**Turn location datasets into useful decisions on a phone.**

Native iOS and Android restroom-finding apps with pedestrian discovery, direction-aware driving recommendations, operating-hours filters, and navigation handoff. A build-time Python workflow prepares **SQLCipher-backed SQLite data with R-Tree spatial indexes**, enabling viewport queries and local filtering. The apps also provide localized interfaces and on-device text translation. Search applies precise distance and travel-heading rules after spatial-index retrieval, including weekday/overnight opening-hours handling and fallback searches for open locations. Bundled database encryption discourages casual extraction; it does not guarantee secrecy against reverse engineering. Android driving search can extend from 15 km to 50 km for open facilities when nearby results are closed; these are geographic distances, not route lengths. The iOS source adds localized, return-from-directions review prompts and a direct rating entry; source changes are distinct from App Store rollout.

### 🌦️ Pocket Weather Station

**Connect on-device measurements to explainable weather information.**

A private **SwiftUI iPhone/iPad and Apple Watch project** combining device barometers, altitude correction, WeatherKit/Open-Meteo forecasts, airport METAR observations and local climate context. Minute-level observations and movement-aware quality checks feed pressure trends and rain-likelihood estimates. Nearby pressure-flow maps, outdoor altitude profiles, widgets/Live Activities and Siri shortcuts connect analysis to daily use; SwiftData records and CSV/ZIP exports support inspection. Saved forecasts can be checked later with Brier scores, reliability plots and paired engine comparisons.

**In development (source version 0.1.0).** Pressure-flow arrival estimates are heuristic and device/provider coverage varies; this is not a certified warning or medical tool. No public source or App Store release is claimed.

## How I approach data work

- **Define the contract.** Make input schemas, units, coordinate systems, and time semantics explicit.
- **Keep decisions inspectable.** Use traceable checks and readable rules so results can be reviewed.
- **Separate checks from conclusions.** Metadata conformity, value screening, and domain accuracy answer different questions.
- **Document the operating context.** Record configuration, dependencies, validation evidence, and recovery paths alongside code.
